Board meeting minutes, 2024-11-20

## Approval of prior minutes

The minutes of the previous regular meeting were reviewed and approved as circulated.

## Staff and committee reports

The executive director summarized operations since the last meeting, including visitor-center attendance and the status of the river-sensor network. The Stewardship Committee reported on easement monitoring visits completed during the period.

## Business

- The Board discussed the high-water trail-closure protocol and recent river-sensor readings.
- The Board discussed visitor-center staffing and seasonal hours.
- The Board discussed trail maintenance on the Ridge Loop.

## Votes

- Motion on the high-water trail-closure protocol and recent river-sensor readings: carried 5–1.
- Motion on visitor-center staffing and seasonal hours: carried 7–0.
- Motion on trail maintenance on the Ridge Loop: carried 5–2.
