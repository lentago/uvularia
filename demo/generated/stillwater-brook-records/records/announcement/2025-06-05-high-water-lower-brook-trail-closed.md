# High water: Lower Brook Trail closed

River-sensor readings at the Route 9 gauge crossed the high-water threshold overnight. The Lower Brook Trail is closed until the water recedes and staff inspect the crossing.

Posted by Stillwater Brook. Current trail and visitor-center status is kept
up to date at https://stillwaterbrook.example.org/status.
