# High water: Ridge Loop ford closed

The river-sensor network shows the brook above the safe ford level. The Ridge Loop ford is closed; use the footbridge route until further notice.

Posted by Stillwater Brook. Current trail and visitor-center status is kept
up to date at https://stillwaterbrook.example.org/status.
