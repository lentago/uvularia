# Visitor center shifts to winter hours

The Watershed Visitor Center is open Saturdays and Sundays, 10 a.m. to 3 p.m., through March. Trails remain open dawn to dusk.

Posted by Stillwater Brook. Current trail and visitor-center status is kept
up to date at https://stillwaterbrook.example.org/status.
