# Board meeting minutes, 2025-11-19

Minutes of the Wednesday, November 19, 2025 meeting of the Stillwater Brook Board of
Trustees. Approved by the Board on 2025-12-17.

**Present:** 7 of 7 trustees. Staff present: Stewardship Coordinator; the Executive Director.

## Approval of prior minutes

The minutes of the previous regular meeting were reviewed and approved as circulated.

## Staff and committee reports

The stewardship coordinator summarized operations since the last meeting, including visitor-center attendance and the status of the river-sensor network. The Stewardship Committee reported on easement monitoring visits completed during the period.

## Business

- The Board discussed the water-quality monitoring partnership with the regional college.
- The Board discussed the annual appeal and the draft operating budget.
- The Board discussed a grant application to the state Division of Ecological Restoration.

## Votes

- Motion on the water-quality monitoring partnership with the regional college: carried 7–0.
- Motion on the annual appeal and the draft operating budget: carried 5–2.
- Motion on a grant application to the state Division of Ecological Restoration: carried 7–0.


The meeting adjourned at 8:45 p.m.. These minutes were approved at the
following regular meeting and posted after that approval.
