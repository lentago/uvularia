# Board meeting minutes, 2026-04-15

Minutes of the Wednesday, April 15, 2026 meeting of the Stillwater Brook Board of
Trustees. Approved by the Board on 2026-05-20.

**Present:** 5 of 7 trustees. Staff present: Outreach Coordinator; the Stewardship Coordinator.

## Approval of prior minutes

The minutes of the previous regular meeting were reviewed and approved as circulated.

## Staff and committee reports

The outreach coordinator summarized operations since the last meeting, including visitor-center attendance and the status of the river-sensor network. The Stewardship Committee reported on easement monitoring visits completed during the period.

## Business

- The Board discussed visitor-center staffing and seasonal hours.
- The Board discussed trail maintenance on the Ridge Loop.

## Votes

- Motion on visitor-center staffing and seasonal hours: carried 7–0.
- Motion on trail maintenance on the Ridge Loop: carried 6–1.


The meeting adjourned at 9:30 p.m.. These minutes were approved at the
following regular meeting and posted after that approval.
