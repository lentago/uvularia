# Board meeting minutes, 2025-09-17

Minutes of the Wednesday, September 17, 2025 meeting of the Stillwater Brook Board of
Trustees. Approved by the Board on 2025-10-15.

**Present:** 5 of 7 trustees. Staff present: Office Manager; the Stewardship Coordinator.

## Approval of prior minutes

The minutes of the previous regular meeting were reviewed and approved as circulated.

## Staff and committee reports

The outreach coordinator summarized operations since the last meeting, including visitor-center attendance and the status of the river-sensor network. The Stewardship Committee reported on easement monitoring visits completed during the period.

## Business

- The Board discussed trail maintenance on the Ridge Loop.
- The Board discussed acceptance of the Hadley parcel donation.
- The Board discussed the water-quality monitoring partnership with the regional college.

## Votes

- Motion on trail maintenance on the Ridge Loop: carried 6–1.
- Motion on acceptance of the Hadley parcel donation: carried 7–0.
- Motion on the water-quality monitoring partnership with the regional college: carried 6–1.


The meeting adjourned at 9:15 p.m.. These minutes were approved at the
following regular meeting and posted after that approval.
