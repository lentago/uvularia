# Board meeting minutes, 2025-05-21

Minutes of the Wednesday, May 21, 2025 meeting of the Stillwater Brook Board of
Trustees. Approved by the Board on 2025-06-18.

**Present:** 7 of 7 trustees. Staff present: Outreach Coordinator; the Stewardship Coordinator.

## Approval of prior minutes

The minutes of the previous regular meeting were reviewed and approved as circulated.

## Staff and committee reports

The outreach coordinator summarized operations since the last meeting, including visitor-center attendance and the status of the river-sensor network. The Stewardship Committee reported on easement monitoring visits completed during the period.

## Business

- The Board discussed a grant application to the state Division of Ecological Restoration.
- The Board discussed stewardship of the Cedar Marsh easement.
- The Board discussed the high-water trail-closure protocol and recent river-sensor readings.

## Votes

- Motion on a grant application to the state Division of Ecological Restoration: carried 5–1.
- Motion on stewardship of the Cedar Marsh easement: carried 6–1.
- Motion on the high-water trail-closure protocol and recent river-sensor readings: carried 7–0.


The meeting adjourned at 9:45 p.m.. These minutes were approved at the
following regular meeting and posted after that approval.
