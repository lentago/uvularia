# Board meeting minutes, 2025-06-18

Minutes of the Wednesday, June 18, 2025 meeting of the Stillwater Brook Board of
Trustees. Approved by the Board on 2025-07-16.

**Present:** 5 of 7 trustees. Staff present: Stewardship Coordinator; the Outreach Coordinator.

## Approval of prior minutes

The minutes of the previous regular meeting were reviewed and approved as circulated.

## Staff and committee reports

The office manager summarized operations since the last meeting, including visitor-center attendance and the status of the river-sensor network. The Stewardship Committee reported on easement monitoring visits completed during the period.

## Business

- The Board discussed stewardship of the Cedar Marsh easement.
- The Board discussed the high-water trail-closure protocol and recent river-sensor readings.

## Votes

- Motion on stewardship of the Cedar Marsh easement: carried 6–0.
- Motion on the high-water trail-closure protocol and recent river-sensor readings: carried 6–0.


The meeting adjourned at 8:15 p.m.. These minutes were approved at the
following regular meeting and posted after that approval.
