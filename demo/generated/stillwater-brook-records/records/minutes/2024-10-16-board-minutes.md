# Board meeting minutes, 2024-10-16

Minutes of the Wednesday, October 16, 2024 meeting of the Stillwater Brook Board of
Trustees. Approved by the Board on 2024-11-20.

**Present:** 5 of 7 trustees. Staff present: Office Manager; the Stewardship Coordinator.

## Approval of prior minutes

The minutes of the previous regular meeting were reviewed and approved as circulated.

## Staff and committee reports

The stewardship coordinator summarized operations since the last meeting, including visitor-center attendance and the status of the river-sensor network. The Stewardship Committee reported on easement monitoring visits completed during the period.

## Business

- The Board discussed stewardship of the Cedar Marsh easement.
- The Board discussed the high-water trail-closure protocol and recent river-sensor readings.

## Votes

- Motion on stewardship of the Cedar Marsh easement: carried 7–0.
- Motion on the high-water trail-closure protocol and recent river-sensor readings: carried 5–0.


The meeting adjourned at 8:45 p.m.. These minutes were approved at the
following regular meeting and posted after that approval.
