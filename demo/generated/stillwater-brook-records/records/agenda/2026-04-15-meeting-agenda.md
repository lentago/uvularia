# April 2026 board meeting agenda

Agenda for the Wednesday, April 15, 2026 meeting of the Stillwater Brook Board of
Trustees.

1. Call to order and roll call
2. Approval of the prior meeting's minutes
3. Staff and committee reports
4. Visitor-center staffing and seasonal hours
5. Trail maintenance: Ridge Loop
6. Public comment
7. Adjournment


Items may be taken out of order. Any member of the public may address the Board
during the public-comment period.
