# April 2025 board meeting agenda

Agenda for the Wednesday, April 16, 2025 meeting of the Stillwater Brook Board of
Trustees.

1. Call to order and roll call
2. Approval of the prior meeting's minutes
3. Staff and committee reports
4. Annual appeal and draft operating budget
5. Grant application: Division of Ecological Restoration
6. Public comment
7. Adjournment


Items may be taken out of order. Any member of the public may address the Board
during the public-comment period.
