# February 2025 board meeting agenda

Agenda for the Wednesday, February 19, 2025 meeting of the Stillwater Brook Board of
Trustees.

1. Call to order and roll call
2. Approval of the prior meeting's minutes
3. Staff and committee reports
4. Proposed acceptance of the Hadley parcel donation
5. Water-quality monitoring partnership
6. Public comment
7. Adjournment


Items may be taken out of order. Any member of the public may address the Board
during the public-comment period.
