# February 2026 board meeting agenda

Agenda for the Wednesday, February 18, 2026 meeting of the Stillwater Brook Board of
Trustees.

1. Call to order and roll call
2. Approval of the prior meeting's minutes
3. Staff and committee reports
4. Stewardship report: Cedar Marsh easement monitoring
5. River-sensor data and the high-water trail-closure protocol
6. Public comment
7. Adjournment


Items may be taken out of order. Any member of the public may address the Board
during the public-comment period.
