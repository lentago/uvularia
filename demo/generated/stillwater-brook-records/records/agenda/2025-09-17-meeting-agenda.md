# September 2025 board meeting agenda

Agenda for the Wednesday, September 17, 2025 meeting of the Stillwater Brook Board of
Trustees.

1. Call to order and roll call
2. Approval of the prior meeting's minutes
3. Staff and committee reports
4. Trail maintenance: Ridge Loop
5. Proposed acceptance of the Hadley parcel donation
6. Water-quality monitoring partnership
7. Public comment
8. Adjournment


Items may be taken out of order. Any member of the public may address the Board
during the public-comment period.
