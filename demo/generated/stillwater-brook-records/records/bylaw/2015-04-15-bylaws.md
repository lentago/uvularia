# Bylaws (2015)

Bylaws of Stillwater Brook Watershed Alliance, a Massachusetts nonprofit corporation, effective
2015-04-15.

These are the original bylaws adopted at incorporation. They were superseded in 2024; this version is retained and marked `superseded`, never deleted.

## Article I — Purpose

To protect the land and water of the watershed through conservation, stewardship, and education.

## Article II — Membership and Trustees

The affairs of the corporation are managed by a Board of up to seven Trustees.
