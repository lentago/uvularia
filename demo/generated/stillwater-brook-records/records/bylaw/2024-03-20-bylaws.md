# Bylaws (2024)

Bylaws of Stillwater Brook Watershed Alliance, a Massachusetts nonprofit corporation, effective
2024-03-20.

The Board amended and restated the bylaws in 2024 to add a conflict-of-interest article and to fix the number of Trustees at seven.

## Article I — Purpose

To protect the land and water of the watershed through conservation, stewardship, and education.

## Article II — Board of Trustees

The Board consists of seven Trustees elected at the annual meeting.

## Article III — Conflicts of Interest

Trustees and staff follow the conflict-of-interest policy, reviewed annually.
