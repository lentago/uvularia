# February 2026 board meeting notice

Stillwater Brook Watershed Alliance (Stillwater Brook) gives notice of a public meeting of its
Board of Trustees.

- **Meeting:** Wednesday, February 18, 2026
- **Time:** 7:00 p.m.
- **Place:** the Watershed Visitor Center, Route 9
- **Attending:** open to the public; remote-participation details are posted at

The agenda is posted as a separate record. Minutes are posted after the Board
approves them at a later meeting. When this notice was posted is recorded in the
